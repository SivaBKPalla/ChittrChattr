ENZO

Why Enzo is the best robot in Crescendo

A short talk about how it was built · about 5 to 7 minutes

ENZO · Crescendo · Team 15771 / 11
Who is Enzo?

Meet the robot

The team

High school students on Team 1577 (Steampunk) built Enzo in Raanana, Israel.

The game

Crescendo is a robot sports game. Robots pick up foam rings called Notes, score points, and climb at the end.

What we’ll talk about

How Enzo was built and why that design is so good. Not game scores.

ENZO · Crescendo · Team 15772 / 11
The game

What robots had to do

  • Pick up Notes from the floor fast
  • Shoot Notes into a big goal called the Speaker
  • Also put Notes in a side goal called the Amp
  • Climb a chain near the end of the match
  • Keep driving even when other robots block them
ENZO · Crescendo · Team 15773 / 11
What “best” means

Not the flashiest — the smartest

  • Has a simple plan for the whole game
  • Picks up and scores Notes without drama
  • Can help its partners in more than one way
  • Gets fixed when something is wrong
  • Is easy for the drivers to use
ENZO · Crescendo · Team 15774 / 11
Making it better

They changed Enzo when they had to

In the middle of the season, the team took Enzo apart and built big parts again.

That’s smart. Good teams don’t stick with a bad first idea. They fix the robot.

ENZO · Crescendo · Team 15775 / 11
Picking up Notes

No Notes = no points

Enzo is built to grab Notes quickly and hold onto them.

In this game, having the Notes is like having the basketball. Enzo was made to win that fight.

ENZO · Crescendo · Team 15776 / 11
Scoring points

Two ways to score

  • Shoot into the Speaker
  • Score in the Amp to help its partners
  • Drivers can choose the better option when it’s crowded
  • Doing both jobs is better than only doing one
ENZO · Crescendo · Team 15777 / 11
End of the match

Climbing without messing up scoring

Some robots focus so much on climbing that they get worse at scoring.

Enzo stays good at scoring during the match, and it can still climb at the end.

The robot’s computer code

Good code makes driving easier

  • Helps the robot aim better
  • Gives drivers simple controls
  • Uses cameras so the robot can “see” the field
  • When drivers trust the robot, they play better
