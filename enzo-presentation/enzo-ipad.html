Team 1577 Steampunk

ENZO

Why this robot is so good

Quick intro

Enzo is Steampunk’s robot

Team 1577 is a high school robotics team from Raanana, Israel. Enzo is the robot they built for Crescendo.

How the team worked

They didn’t freeze the first design

Mid-season, they ripped out big chunks of Enzo and rebuilt them.

The intake

Rollers in front pull Notes inside

Notes are the foam rings on the floor. Enzo has spinning rollers on the front. Drive into a Note, and those rollers yank it up into the robot and keep it from falling back out.

The shooter

Same robot, two scoring paths

Once a Note is inside, Enzo can shoot it into the Speaker, or place it in the Amp for its partners.

The climb

They fitted climbing around scoring

Enzo can hang on the chain at the end. The climb doesn’t wreck the scoring setup, so scoring still works the whole match.

The code

The computer side does the annoying work

Cameras and code help Enzo aim and keep controls simple for the drivers.

Why it adds up

The parts were built to fit together

  • Intake feeds the shooter cleanly
  • Shooter can still work even with a climb on the robot
  • Code makes those parts easier to use in a real match
  • When something was weak, the team rebuilt it
