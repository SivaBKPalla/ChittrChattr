# Speaker script (~5–6 minutes, 9th-grade friendly)

No match scores, rankings, or awards.

---

**Slide 1 — Title (30s)**  
Hi — today I’m talking about **Enzo**, a robot from FRC Team 1577, Steampunk. I’m going to explain why Enzo is the best robot for the Crescendo game by looking at how it was designed — not by looking at scores.

**Slide 2 — Meet Enzo (30s)**  
Enzo was built by students in Raanana, Israel. Crescendo is like a sports game for robots: pick up foam rings called Notes, score them, and climb at the end. Our focus is smart design.

**Slide 3 — The challenge (40s)**  
Robots had to grab Notes, shoot into the Speaker, score in the Amp, climb a chain, and keep moving when other robots blocked them. That’s a lot — so the best robot has to be good at the whole game.

**Slide 4 — What “best” means (35s)**  
Best doesn’t mean flashiest. It means: clear plan, smooth scoring, helpful in more than one way, willing to improve, and easy to drive.

**Slide 5 — Rebuild (35s)**  
Mid-season, the team rebuilt big parts of Enzo. That’s a good thing. Great teams fix what isn’t working instead of sticking with a bad first idea.

**Slide 6 — Notes (30s)**  
If you can’t pick up Notes, you can’t score. Owning Notes is like owning the basketball. Enzo was built to grab and control them well.

**Slide 7 — Scoring (35s)**  
Enzo can score two ways: Speaker shots and Amp scoring. That flexibility helps when the field is crowded.

**Slide 8 — Climbing (30s)**  
Some robots focus so hard on climbing that scoring gets worse. Enzo stays good at scoring and can still climb at the end.

**Slide 9 — Software (35s)**  
Good code makes hard jobs feel easy — better aiming, clearer controls, and sensors that help the robot “see.” When drivers trust the robot, they play smarter.

**Slide 10 — Why Enzo (45s)**  
Five reasons: built for the whole game, rebuilt when needed, great at Notes, scores more than one way, and software that helps drivers.

**Slide 11 — Close (20s + Q&A)**  
Enzo is best because of how it was built: clear plan, willing to improve, easy to drive, ready for the whole game. Questions?
