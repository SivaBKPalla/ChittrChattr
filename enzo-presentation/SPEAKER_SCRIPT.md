# Speaker script

Talk about the robot. No scores. No long rules lesson.

---

**1 — Title**  
This is Enzo, from Team 1577 Steampunk. I’m going to talk about why this robot is so good — not the scores, just how they built it.

**2 — Intro**  
Steampunk is a high school team from Raanana, Israel. Enzo is their Crescendo robot. I’ll go through how the main parts work, then why that matters.

**3 — How the team worked**  
Mid-season they ripped out big chunks and rebuilt them. That’s them saying “this isn’t good enough” and fixing it. A lot of teams won’t do that.

**4 — Intake**  
Notes are the foam rings on the floor. Enzo has spinning rollers on the front. Drive into a Note and the rollers pull it inside and hold it. Drivers can scoop Notes while moving — they don’t need a perfect lineup every time.

**5 — Shooter**  
Once a Note is in, Enzo can shoot it into the Speaker or put it in the Amp for partners. Two options help when the field is crowded.

**6 — Climb**  
Enzo can hang on the chain, but the climb was fitted so scoring still works. Some teams bolt on a climb late and wreck everything else.

**7 — Code**  
Cameras and code handle aiming and keep controls simple. Drivers can play the match instead of fighting the robot.

**8 — Why it adds up**  
Intake feeds the shooter. Climb doesn’t kill scoring. Code makes it usable. And the team kept fixing weak parts.

**9 — Close**
